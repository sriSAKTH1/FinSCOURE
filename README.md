# FinScore — Fix Your Finance With Me!

> **FinScore** is a personal financial health assessment and wealth diagnostic platform. It translates complex personal finances into an intuitive 0–1000 credit-score-style financial fitness score, dynamic diagnostic analytics, actionable prescriptions, and financial calculators.

---

## 🌟 What FinScore Does

Most people track expenses or check their credit score, but traditional credit scores only measure how good someone is at borrowing money and paying interest. **FinScore measures actual financial health, independence, and resilience.**

By assessing income, expenditures, liabilities, insurance safety nets, emergency reserves, asset diversification, and life milestones, FinScore produces an executive-grade financial diagnostic with zero jargon.

---

## 🚀 Core Features & Present Capabilities

### 1. Comprehensive 8-Step Financial Diagnostic
A guided, structured wizard that captures the complete financial picture in minutes:
- **Step 1: Personal & Demographics** — Name, age, dependents, occupation, currency preference (₹ INR, $ USD, € EUR, £ GBP), and monthly take-home salary.
- **Step 2: Monthly Cash Flow & Outflows** — Fixed living expenses, discretionary lifestyle spending, active loan EMIs, rent, and monthly savings.
- **Step 3: Debt & Liabilities Audit** — Detailed breakdown of home loans, vehicle loans, education loans, personal loans, and high-interest credit card debt with interest rates and EMIs.
- **Step 4: Insurance & Protection Shield** — Term life insurance coverage, health insurance sum insured, policy types, and family health protection adequacy.
- **Step 5: Emergency Liquidity Buffer** — Liquid savings across savings accounts, liquid mutual funds, and fixed deposits measured against monthly burn rate.
- **Step 6: Assets & Net Worth Inventory** — Equity mutual funds, direct stocks, EPF/PPF/NPS, physical gold, real estate, bank deposits, and alternative investments.
- **Step 7: Life Goals & Milestones** — Timelines, target amounts, and monthly allocations for retirement, home buying, children's higher education, wedding, or wealth creation.
- **Step 8: Review & Instant Audit** — Summary validation before triggering the proprietary scoring engine.

---

### 2. The 0–1000 FinScore Algorithm & 6 Health Pillars
Evaluates financial vitality across six foundational dimensions, each scored independently:
1. **Savings & Budgeting Health (Weight: 20%)** — Measures savings rate (ideal: 30%+ of net take-home) and discretionary expense discipline.
2. **Debt & Leverage Control (Weight: 20%)** — Debt-to-Income (DTI) ratio scoring, punishing toxic high-interest credit card debt and penalizing EMIs exceeding 40% of income.
3. **Protection & Risk Management (Weight: 15%)** — Life insurance adequacy (target: 10x–15x annual income) and medical coverage sanity checks.
4. **Emergency Preparedness (Weight: 15%)** — Runway adequacy in months (ideal: 6+ months of mandatory expenses in safe liquid instruments).
5. **Investment & Wealth Velocity (Weight: 15%)** — Equity-to-debt balance, long-term wealth compounding vehicles, and asset diversity.
6. **Goal Preparedness & Planning (Weight: 15%)** — Realistic milestone horizon tracking and committed monthly contributions toward major life goals.

**Rating Tiers:**
- `850 – 1000`: **Exceptional** (Financially resilient, compounding wealth efficiently)
- `700 – 849`: **Strong** (Solid foundation with minor optimization opportunities)
- `550 – 699`: **Moderate** (Adequate cash flow, but vulnerable to emergencies or under-invested)
- `400 – 549`: **At Risk** (High debt-to-income, insufficient safety nets)
- `0 – 399`: **Critical** (Immediate intervention needed on debt restructuring and cash burn)

### 4. Complete 3×3 Suite of 9 Financial Calculators
Interactive wealth projection engines featuring dual-axis charts (Years on X-axis, Amount on Y-axis) and year-by-year cash breakdown tables:
1. **SIP Calculator (Systematic Investment Plan)** — Models monthly compounding, total invested capital vs estimated market returns.
2. **Step-up SIP Calculator** — Demonstrates the compounding impact of annual investment percentage increments aligned with salary appraisals.
3. **Lumpsum Investment Calculator** — Projects one-time wealth creation over custom horizons with varying CAGR rates.
4. **SWP Calculator (Systematic Withdrawal Plan)** — Models post-retirement monthly cash payouts, remaining principal balance, and capital longevity.
5. **Retirement Corpus Planner** — Reverse-calculates required retirement nest egg based on current lifestyle expenses, inflation, life expectancy, and post-retirement yield.
6. **CAGR Calculator (Compound Annual Growth Rate)** — Computes exact annualized compounded rate of return across multi-year asset purchases.
7. **Fixed Deposit (FD) Calculator** — Models term deposits with quarterly, monthly, or annual compounding and cumulative maturity values.
8. **Loan EMI & Amortization Calculator** — Calculates exact monthly payments, total cumulative interest, and principal-vs-interest distribution.
9. **Inflation Impact Calculator** — Forecasts the erosion of purchasing power over 5 to 30 years at user-specified inflation rates.

### 🧠 1. AI-Powered Financial Co-Pilot
- **Conversational Financial Advisor** — Context-aware AI assistant capable of answering questions such as *"Can I afford a ₹15 Lakh car loan right now?"* or *"How does prepaying ₹50,000 against my home loan affect my retirement date?"*
- **Natural Language Document Ingestion** — Automatic extraction of financial data by uploading bank account statements (PDF), credit card bills, and mutual fund CAS (Consolidated Account Statements).
- **Behavioral Spending Nudges** — Personalized insights detecting lifestyle inflation or recurring subscription leaks.

### 🔗 2. Open Banking & Account Aggregator (AA) Integration
- **Zero-Friction Auto-Population** — Direct integration with RBI-regulated Account Aggregators (in India) and Plaid/Open Banking (in US/Europe) to fetch net balances, investments, and debts with explicit consent.
- **Live Net Worth Synchronization** — Auto-refreshing portfolio valuations for stocks, mutual funds, provident funds, and real estate benchmarks.
- **Credit Bureau Linking** — Dual-perspective dashboard displaying traditional CIBIL/Experian score alongside the holistic FinScore.

### 🎯 3. Advanced Scenario Modeling ("What-If" Engine)
- **Economic Stress Testing** — Simulate personal financial resilience against external shocks:
  - Job loss or income reduction for 6–12 months.
  - Stock market correction (-20% to -40%) on retirement runway.
  - Sizable medical emergency not covered by base policy.
- **Debt Payoff Accelerators** — Interactive comparison of **Debt Avalanche** (highest interest first) vs **Debt Snowball** (smallest balance first) strategies with exact payoff dates and total interest saved.
- **Early Retirement / FIRE Simulator** — Granular Financial Independence, Retire Early (FIRE) modeling factoring in safe withdrawal rates (3.5%–4%), healthcare buffers, and coast-FIRE horizons.

### 👥 4. Family & Household Consolidated Mode
- **Joint Household Balance Sheets** — Aggregate spouse or family members' cash flows, combined loan commitments, and shared goals into a unified family FinScore.
- **Generational Wealth Planning** — Estate planning checklists, nominee verification audits, and term insurance coverage cross-matched with total household dependents.

### 📱 5. Native Mobile Experience & Progressive Web App (PWA)
- **Offline Assessment Mode** — Complete financial audits and run all 9 financial calculators completely offline with local client-side encryption.
- **Milestone Push Alerts** — Timely reminders for SIP renewal dates, emergency fund replenishment triggers, and annual policy reviews.
- **Biometric App Lock** — Face ID / Fingerprint authentication ensuring confidential personal financial data remains private on mobile devices.

### 🌐 6. Multi-Currency & Cross-Border Wealth Optimization
- **NRI / Expat Wealth Mode** — Dual-currency financial tracking for expatriates and non-resident professionals managing foreign income alongside home-country assets and liabilities.
- **Tax Regime Comparison** — Built-in tax bracket evaluation showing net take-home implications under Old vs New tax regimes.
- **Encrypted Local Export** — One-click password-protected JSON/Excel export with zero cloud storage required, keeping 100% of sensitive financial data in the user's custody.

## 🛡️ Privacy & Philosophy

- **Zero-Knowledge by Design**: Financial records and calculations run locally in the browser runtime.
- **Client-Side Persistence**: Drafts are maintained in encrypted local browser storage without requiring logins or collecting personal identity documents.
- **Educational Empowerment**: FinScore provides objective, transparent mathematical analysis to help users make informed decisions rather than pushing biased commission-driven financial products.
